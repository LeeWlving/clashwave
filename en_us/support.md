# Support and contact

## Get help

Open an issue through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/LeeWlving/clashwave/issues). Include the platform, operating-system version, ClashWave version, reproduction steps, and relevant logs with server addresses and other sensitive details removed.

Never post subscription URLs, access tokens, proxy passwords, signing keys, or other credentials publicly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ClashWave provide proxy nodes?

No. ClashWave is a client. Profiles, subscriptions, and proxy services are selected by the user.

### How do I stop the Android VPN?

Tap Stop on the ClashWave home screen or use the disconnect action in Android's ongoing notification.

### How do I delete my data?

Remove subscriptions and profiles in the app, or open Android App info → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Uninstalling the app also deletes local application data stored by Android.

### Where can I download the app?

See [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/LeeWlving/clashwave/releases). Pre-release builds may be available only through Google Play testing tracks.
